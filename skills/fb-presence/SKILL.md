# Facebook presence

Read [runtime](references/runtime.md), [surfaces](references/surfaces.md), and
[presence checklist](references/presence.md). Use [privacy](references/privacy.md)
before proposing public personal information; [growth](references/growth.md)
helps define a coherent reason to follow.

Establish ordinary profile, professional mode, or Page; intended audience,
purpose, current fields/screenshots, actual credentials/offer, and privacy
boundaries. Inspect only supplied or authorized accessible information. Don't
claim to have seen an inaccessible banner, pinned post, link, or setting.

Review clarity of identity, audience fit, editorial promise, proof, contact
path, visual consistency, relevant featured content, and what public viewers
can actually see. Separate confirmed issues from missing evidence. Prioritize
fixes by user goal and available controls, not a universal 100-point rubric.

Rewrite only applicable fields within verified limits. Do not assume LinkedIn
headline/about layouts, Instagram bio limits, or a universal Facebook action
button. Recommend a banner/image brief when helpful, not a nonexistent finished
asset. Check links when accessible or mark them unverified. Avoid unsupported
credentials, private contact exposure, and promises the content cannot fulfill.

Return prioritized changes, revised copy, asset recommendations, privacy/feature
checks, and a next action. Don't switch professional mode, change account names,
edit Page settings, grant team access, or publish. Route post performance to
`fb-audit` and content scheduling to `fb-plan` when available.
