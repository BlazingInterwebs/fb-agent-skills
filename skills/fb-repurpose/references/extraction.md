# Extract the native conversation

Build an evidence map, not a condensed chapter list.

| Pull | Question to ask | Possible derivative |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Situation | What would the reader recognize from their own life/work? | A concrete story or discussion |
| Decision | What changed, what trade-off mattered, and why? | Build-in-public or considered opinion |
| Mechanism | What explanation helps someone act differently? | Educational text, diagram brief, demonstration |
| Observation | What was actually seen and what is still unknown? | Experiment update with limitations |
| Mistake | What can be disclosed honestly and usefully? | Lesson, not fabricated vulnerability |
| Evidence | Which numbers/examples are substantiated and permissioned? | Scoped case study, not inflated result |
| Open question | What answer would genuinely help the creator or reader? | Relevant conversation, not reaction bait |

## Standalone check

Supply who/what matters, why the situation is consequential, and the useful
payoff. Remove source-dependent references unless explained. Do not transplant
a LinkedIn authority monologue into a private group, or turn an Instagram
caption into Facebook copy without reconsidering audience and format.
For a video clip, use supplied verified timecodes and enough setup to prevent
misrepresentation. Without timecodes, identify a passage rather than fake cuts.

## Original hypothetical example

Source: a complete business podcast describes marking required onboarding
fields after three testers were confused; a second test is pending.

- Feed story: what the testers encountered and the decision to stop making
  people guess; no invented conversion outcome.
- Image brief: annotated form before/after, using owned/redacted screenshots.
- Video: demonstrate the distinction and explain the reasoning.
- Group contribution: discuss confusing onboarding signals only if relevant
  to the group's purpose and promotional rules.
- Story: an honest small progress update, not a fake launch celebration.

These are opportunities, not a requirement to publish all five or invent assets.
