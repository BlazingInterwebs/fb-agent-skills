# Facebook repurposing

Read [runtime](references/runtime.md), [privacy](references/privacy.md) before
using source material, and [extraction](references/extraction.md). Use
[surfaces](references/surfaces.md) for formats and [growth](references/growth.md)
for audience-growth objectives.

## Extract before composing

Read the complete available source. If only an excerpt is accessible, identify
its boundaries and do not imply full-source coverage. Establish source rights,
intended audience, objective, and what may be made public. A business event
needs an actual account of what happened; don't manufacture scenes.

Extract situations, consequential decisions, useful explanations, demonstrations,
supported observations/results, mistakes, and genuine questions. For each,
record source location, facts versus interpretation, missing context, permission,
and possible Facebook reader value. A quotable line alone may be too thin.
Do not force a fixed number of derivatives from an insufficient source.

Choose the native format for each opportunity. A recognizable situation may
support a conversation post; a demonstration may need video; a process change
may benefit from images; a timely detail may support Stories. Group versions
require local rules and role. Reuse the underlying idea, not automatically the
other platform's pacing, formatting, CTA or promotional framing.

## Handoff and output

Deliver an opportunity map with source location, independent promise/payoff,
new context needed, surface/format, verified claims, rights status, and optional
business connection. Rank by relevance and feasibility, not raw derivative
count. Draft the selected outputs when requested using available `fb-post`,
`fb-video`, or `fb-story` instructions. Without siblings, supply scoped text
briefs rather than promising their scripts/rendering.

Each finished derivative must work for someone who never sees the source.
Check that it adds a complete explanation, useful decision, demonstration,
or considered conversation. Avoid 'as I said in my video' as a substitute for
context, but include source credit/link when appropriate. Preserve factual
scope; leave unconfirmed claims outside final copy. No footage downloads,
copyrighted asset reuse, private-call disclosure, or publishing is implied.
