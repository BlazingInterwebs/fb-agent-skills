# Facebook Groups

Read [runtime](references/runtime.md), [privacy](references/privacy.md), and
[group modes](references/group-modes.md). Use [surfaces](references/surfaces.md)
for public/private visibility and feature checks.

Establish group purpose, actual rules, user's role/identity, public/private
status, intended contribution, and necessary source permissions. Ask for rules
when missing and material. Don't assume member access gives admin privileges,
permission to promote, or consent to reuse others' stories outside the group.

Choose member contribution, admin discussion/onboarding, or moderation advice.
Develop a specific useful question, firsthand lesson, explanation, welcome,
or response that serves this community. A Group is not just another slot for
the user's Page copy. Remove unsolicited offer/link framing where rules or
context disallow it. Don't invent community traditions, members' opinions,
or authority the user doesn't have.

For growth, prioritize a recognizable community purpose, useful recurring
discussions, member experience, and evidence of healthy participation, not
mass invitations or harvested contacts. Do not transfer profile follower-growth
claims to membership growth. For moderation, recommend proportionate actions
using actual rules and explain uncertainty; never delete, ban, or impersonate.

Return the scoped contribution/admin material, relevant rule checks, and
permissions still needed. Use `fb-post` for composition after constraints are
clear, `fb-reply` for owned discussion responses, and `fb-audit` for authorized
group activity analysis. Without siblings, provide the supported draft and
state limitations. No cross-group blasting, private post export, or publishing.
