## Observed evidence

[Buffer's 2026 report](https://buffer.com/resources/state-of-social-media-engagement-2026/)
uses its provider dataset and predominantly 2025 publication data. Facebook
image, video, and text engagement rates were relatively close within that
cohort. Do not generalize the sample to all profiles/Groups or every objective.
[Buffer's reply analysis](https://buffer.com/resources/replying-to-your-comments-on-facebook-boosts-engagement/)
associated replies with about 9.5% more reactions, not proven additional reach,
causal uplift, or conversions. These findings motivate tests, not prescriptions.

## Hypotheses and uncertainty

The growth loop, format choices, opening options, cadence, and sampling minimums
are product heuristics unless supported by the user's actual comparable data.
No platform ranking weights, best-time rules, or fixed viral thresholds are
established here. Live retention, composer features, Story tools, insights,
recommendation eligibility, and regional availability can change.

Several current Transparency/Help/Messenger developer pages were blocked or
login-gated during research. V1 does not claim current Messenger API windows,
all analytics fields, every interactive Story feature, or universal organic
carousel support. Validate these before adding an integration or technical rule.

For findings, attach URL/file, captured date, publication date when known,
surface, format, metric definition, sample, method, paid/organic status,
limitations, and one of: official guidance / observed evidence / hypothesis.
