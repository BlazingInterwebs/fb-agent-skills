# Acknowledgments and provenance

Jake Schincariol's independently published skill collections informed the
architectural study behind this project:

- [YouTube](https://github.com/Jakeschincariol/chatgpt-youtube-agent-skill)
- [Instagram](https://github.com/Jakeschincariol/instagram-agent-skill)
- [LinkedIn](https://github.com/Jakeschincariol/linkedin-agent-skill)

We learned from modular capability names, explicit workflow contracts,
progressive references, deterministic helpers, and practical handoffs.
Facebook Agent Skills instructions, examples, rubrics, and helpers were authored
independently. No upstream skill content, hook library, script, or lexicon was
copied into this repository. The inspected upstream collections use MIT.

Meta sources are linked and summarized in the [evidence register](shared/evidence.md).
Buffer research is treated as cohort-specific observation, not platform policy.
The project supplies no Meta assets, copyrighted creator media, proprietary
datasets, private recordings, or account exports.

If a contribution incorporates third-party material, identify its source,
license and scope, retain required notices, and get maintainer review. Don't
copy manuals or another creator's personal examples into a skill pack.
