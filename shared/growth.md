# Audience growth without engagement bait

Audience growth is a first-class objective alongside community and business
outcomes. It is not a guarantee or a command to make every post promotional.

## Creation loop

Audience need -> original concept -> strong opening -> satisfying payoff ->
reason to return -> measured iteration.

1. Name the reader's specific situation, question, desired outcome, or tension.
   Distinguish supplied audience evidence from an assumed persona.
2. Choose an angle the creator can support: firsthand experience, demonstration,
   useful explanation, verified result, or a considered opinion. Don't borrow
   another creator's personal story or manufacture a controversial claim.
3. Develop openings appropriate to the format: a recognizable situation,
   consequential decision, concrete demonstration, genuine question, or result
   with essential context. Use accurate stakes; avoid clickbait withheld payoff.
4. Deliver the opening's promise. Make the lesson, evidence, explanation, or
   demonstration useful independently of following, commenting, or buying.
5. Give a natural reason to return: a recognizable topic, ongoing experiment,
   next genuinely useful episode, or coherent series. A follow request is
   optional. Don't substitute a CTA for a weak piece.
6. Choose an observable success measure and record what can actually be tested.
   Feed measured findings back into topics, openings, pacing, and allocation.

## Editorial checks, not predictive scores

Can an unfamiliar reader understand the situation? Is the first line/frame
specific and truthful? Does the body fulfill it? Is there something worth
remembering or sharing? Does the piece fit this surface and this creator?
Would the conversation invitation still be useful without algorithm rewards?
Report a concrete weakness and correction, not a fake likelihood of virality.

## Measurement

When supplied: non-follower reach/distribution, watch time/retention, observed
shares, content-attributed follows, and net account follower growth. Define
each metric and window. Net growth can combine many posts and other activity;
it does not establish that one post caused it. Views per follower do not prove
non-follower reach. Qualitative questions and returning participants can be
useful observations, but do not label them analytics unless measured.

No magic posting times, mandatory engagement rounds, fixed hook library IDs,
or platform-wide follower conversion benchmarks. Test coherent series and
standalone posts against the account's own comparable evidence.
