# Facebook posts

Read [runtime](references/runtime.md). Use [surface checks](references/surfaces.md)
when choosing presentation, [growth](references/growth.md) for audience-growth
briefs, and [privacy](references/privacy.md) when source material is sensitive.
Choose intent using [post modes](references/post-modes.md); load only the
relevant mode, not a fixed hook formula for every request.

## Develop the piece

Establish surface/visibility, intended reader, objective, and the actual facts.
If these are absent, make a labeled provisional draft rather than inventing
first-person experience. Identify one useful situation, decision, lesson,
demonstration, or conversation. Pick text, single image, multi-image, or link
based on what the reader needs to understand. A supplied format remains the
default unless it cannot support the requested result.

For discovery, make the situation legible to strangers. Develop a few genuinely
different opening directions when requested or when selecting an angle would
help; otherwise draft directly. Make the body fulfill the opening with specific
context, evidence, or explanation. Avoid mechanically short one-sentence lines,
manufactured suspense, or a mandatory question at the end.

Match voice to supplied samples. Keep authentic vocabulary, humor, and cadence.
If a claim needs evidence, either omit it, qualify it, or visibly mark it for
verification; never quietly publish a placeholder as a fact. A CTA should
advance the reader's goal and the user's objective, not merely request signals.
No CTA is a valid result. Route explicit voice revision to `fb-human` if available.

## Deliver

Return final copy separated from editorial notes. Add an image/sequence brief
or link-preview instructions only when applicable. State assumptions and
unverified claims outside the publishable copy. For growth or experiments,
briefly identify the payoff, reason to return, and available measure of success.
Do not claim a post will go viral or is technically upload-ready without assets.

## Handoffs

Use `fb-video` for a spoken script; `fb-story` for a Story sequence;
`fb-group` first when rules/role materially change a group post; `fb-plan` for a
calendar; `fb-audit` for results. Without a sibling, give only the scoped draft
or brief supported here and say what remains. Never expand a one-post request
into a campaign, or publish the draft.
