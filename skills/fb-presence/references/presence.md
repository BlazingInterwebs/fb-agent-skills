# Presence checklist by surface

## Personal/professional-mode profile

Represent the individual truthfully. Clarify whom their public content helps
without replacing their identity with keyword spam. Review supplied intro,
public biography/details, cover/profile imagery, and any available featured
content. Ask what information they want public; don't reveal contact/location
or personal history merely to make a profile look complete. Professional mode
adds creator capabilities but doesn't mean Page-style team access is available.

## Page

Clarify business/creator identity, whom it serves, accurate service information,
substantiated proof, appropriate contact route and links, and applicable cover,
profile, featured/pinned content, categories or action controls. Verify current
fields and limits. A local business may need useful hours/service-area details;
a creator may need an editorial promise. Do not force the same checklist weights.

## Prioritization

Return confirmed obstacle, user impact, suggested change, evidence, and any
dependency. An inaccessible field is not automatically a failing score.
For growth, ask whether a stranger can tell what following will provide.
For business, ask whether someone can take a legitimate next step. Respect
privacy and verification even when a more aggressive change might look persuasive.
