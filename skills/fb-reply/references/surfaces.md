# Facebook surfaces and feature checks

Last researched: 2026-10-08. Announcements describe capabilities and rollouts,
not a guarantee that a specific account has every feature today.

| Surface | Content context | Evidence boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ordinary profile | Personal identity; audience selector matters; public and friends-only content are different briefs | Use supplied/visible responses. Do not assume professional analytics |
| Professional-mode profile | A person's existing profile with creator tools; public creator content can coexist with restricted posts | Use actual Professional Dashboard fields/screenshots. Do not assume Page team roles |
| Page | Public business/creator presence; delegated access depends on assigned roles | Use actual Page/Business Suite exports; never infer authorization from knowing its URL |
| Group | Shared community; member/admin roles, rules, post approval, and public/private status matter | Member access is not admin analytics or permission to reuse members' stories |

For growth briefs, distinguish existing relationships from people who do not
know the creator. Give unfamiliar readers enough context without pretending
every post is public. Never change privacy settings for distribution.

## Formats

- Text/image/link posts: choose intent first, then format. A link post must
  still tell readers what is useful at the destination. No universal rule
  requires hiding links in comments, avoiding links, or using a hashtag quota.
- Multi-image content: provide an image sequence and captions when appropriate.
  Confirm the current organic composer/presentation. Do not promise LinkedIn
  document uploads or Instagram carousel mechanics. Ads carousels are outside V1.
- Video/Reels: Meta announced unified new-video sharing as Reels, with short
  and long lengths and flexible formats. Confirm current upload requirements;
  don't impose a universal short duration or 9:16 ratio on all Facebook video.
- Stories: verify audience, available links/stickers, duration, safe zones,
  caption tools, and insights in the user's current account. Do not import
  Instagram interactive features or analytics as guaranteed Facebook tools.
- Live: prepare agenda, demonstrations, moderation notes, and clip candidates;
  no broadcasting. Meta announced a 30-day retention policy for new profile/Page
  Live recordings. Recommend authorized preservation of owned originals, but
  do not download or transfer them unless requested.
- Messenger: personal and Page messaging are different contexts. Drafting is
  supported; automated sending, API windows, and permissions are not supplied
  by this pack. Verify current official requirements before a separate integration.
- Groups/Forum: local rules and community needs remain the brief. Forum is an
  evolving Groups-related surface, not a bundled capability or permission.
